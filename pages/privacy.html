Privacy policy

What we collect

Only what you type: username, hashed password, optional profile info, posts. No email, phone, IP, device or analytics data.

Where it is stored

In your browser's localStorage. There is no server. Your login session uses sessionStorage and ends when the tab closes.

Your control

Export or delete everything from Settings. Clearing your browser data also wipes it.

Prototype notice

Because data is local, accounts and posts are visible only on this device and browser. A real launch would need end-to-end encryption and a hardened backend.
